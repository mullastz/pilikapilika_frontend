Compare air cargo and sea cargo options

Start with your goods and delivery needs. Check an agent's listed transport methods, then ask about the specific China-to-Tanzania service they can arrange.

Questions about air freight

If an agent offers air cargo, ask whether your items are accepted, how the chargeable weight is calculated, and which collection and delivery points the quote covers. Request the current departure arrangements and an estimated arrival date.

Questions about sea freight

If an agent offers sea cargo, ask how your goods will be measured and charged, when cargo is collected, and how shipment updates are provided. Confirm the destination handling and collection or delivery arrangements in Tanzania.

Get a clear China to Tanzania cargo quote

A useful quote explains the full service for your goods. Share the same shipment details with each agent so you can compare what is included.

  • Describe the goods, number of items, packaging, weight, and dimensions.
  • Provide the collection location in China and your destination in Tanzania.
  • Confirm whether the price is per kilogram, per cubic metre (CBM), per item, or for the whole shipment.
  • Check the currency and ask which collection, transport, handling, and final delivery charges are included.
  • Ask the agent to explain any documentation, clearance arrangements, or additional charges that apply to your shipment.

Agree on these details with your agent before booking or sending goods to a collection point. Prices and service availability depend on the agent and shipment.
